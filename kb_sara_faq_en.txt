FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ) — SUMBANGAN ASAS RAHMAH (SARA) FOR ALL
Issued by the Ministry of Finance Malaysia, 5 February 2026

---

SECTION A: INTRODUCTION

Question 1: What is Sumbangan Asas Rahmah (SARA) for All?
Answer: The Prime Minister announced that the SARA for All assistance of RM100 will be distributed as a one-off payment on 9 February 2026 via MyKad to all Malaysian citizens aged 18 and above, to help with Ramadan preparations and Chinese New Year celebrations.

Question 2: Who is eligible to receive SARA for All?
Answer: Eligibility for SARA for All is limited to:
- Malaysian citizens
- Individuals aged 18 and above in the year 2026

Question 3: Do I need to apply for SARA for All?
Answer: No. Eligibility for SARA for All is automatic based on MyKad holder data of Malaysian citizens as of 23 January 2026 from the National Registration Department (JPN).

Question 4: When and how will SARA for All be implemented?
Answer: Recipients of SARA for All will receive essential goods assistance on a cashless basis, automatically credited into their MyKad. Basic groceries from approved product categories can be purchased at selected supermarkets and grocery stores nationwide using MyKad.

SARA for All is a one-off credit of RM100 that can be spent from 9 February 2026 until 31 December 2026.

Collectively, a household can receive a higher total SARA credit. For example, a family consisting of a married couple and three children aged 18 and above will receive a total of RM500, not just RM100.

For existing SARA STR recipients, the February 2026 existing SARA entitlement will be credited to MyKad in early February 2026, while the additional one-off RM100 will be credited on 9 February 2026. Both existing and one-off SARA for All credits can be spent until 31 December 2026.

Question 5: How much is the SARA for All entitlement for eligible recipients?
Answer: Individual MyKad holders who are adult Malaysian citizens aged 18 and above (born in 2008 or earlier) will receive a one-off distribution of RM100 on 9 February 2026. This can be spent from 9 February 2026 until 31 December 2026.

Question 6: How can I check my SARA for All eligibility?
Answer: Eligibility can be checked through:
- The official SARA portal at https://sara.gov.my (from 9 February 2026)
- Contact MyKasih: 03-7720 1800 (operating hours: 9.00 AM - 5.00 PM)
If you are not listed as a SARA for All recipient, please submit your enquiry via email to sara@treasury.gov.my

Question 7: I am a Malaysian citizen but do not yet have a MyKad. Can I still receive SARA for All?
Answer: If you meet the eligibility requirements for SARA for All but do not have a MyKad, you need to visit the National Registration Department (JPN) to obtain your MyKad. Purchases using SARA for All can be made up to 31 December 2026 after your MyKad has been issued.

---

SECTION B: IMPLEMENTATION OF SARA FOR ALL

Question 8: Where can I find instructions on how to purchase SARA goods using MyKad?
Answer: You can watch the tutorial video provided on YouTube: https://youtu.be/a1YQ25pW0p8

Question 9: When will SARA for All be distributed to recipients?
Answer: Recipients of SARA for All will receive a one-off credit of RM100 via MyKad on 9 February 2026 to purchase selected essential goods. The validity period for SARA for All is from 9 February 2026 until 31 December 2026.

Question 10: Where can I purchase essential goods under this program?
Answer: Eligible recipients can purchase selected essential goods at more than 10,000 SARA merchant partners nationwide including Mydin, Giant, Econsave, The Store, Pacific, Milimewa, 99 Speedmart, Lotus Stores (Tesco), as well as independent supermarkets and mini markets. The number of SARA merchant partners is not final and will increase over time. The list of participating supermarkets and grocery stores can be checked at the official SARA portal at https://sara.gov.my

Question 11: Can I make purchases at supermarkets and grocery stores outside my residential area?
Answer: Yes. You can purchase at any SARA merchant partner nationwide.

Question 12: What essential goods can I buy?
Answer: Recipients can purchase essential goods from 15 approved product categories:
1. Rice
2. Bread
3. Eggs
4. Cooking oil
5. Flour
6. Biscuits
7. Noodles
8. Beverages (e.g., milk, tea, coffee, baby milk)
9. Canned food (e.g., sardines, tuna)
10. Seasonings and condiments (e.g., sauce, soy sauce, sugar, salt)
11. Personal care products (e.g., toothpaste, sanitary pads, soap, baby diapers)
12. Household cleaning products (e.g., laundry detergent, dishwashing liquid, floor cleaner)
13. Over-the-counter medicine (approved by the Ministry of Health)
14. Frozen food
15. School supplies (e.g., school uniforms, shoes, school bags, stationery)

Essential goods under the SARA program will be labelled for recipients' reference.

Question 13: How do I purchase essential goods?
Answer: The purchase process is as follows:
Step 1 — Select essential goods from the 15 approved product categories that have the MyKasih label on the price tag shelf.
Step 2 — Show your MyKad for verification. The cashier will check the MyKad status of the recipient.
Step 3 — The cashier scans the barcode of each essential item you wish to purchase according to the approved categories.
Step 4 — Pay using MyKad. The recipient uses their MyKad to confirm the purchase transaction.
Step 5 — Keep the receipt for reference. The recipient should keep the receipt to review the purchase transaction.

Question 14: How can I check my SARA for All balance in my MyKad account?
Answer: The SARA balance in your MyKad account can be checked from 9 February 2026 through:
- The official SARA portal: https://sara.gov.my
- The MyKasih app (available on Google Play Store and App Store)
- The purchase receipt (showing the current balance before and after purchase)

Question 15: Do I need to pay the excess amount if my purchase exceeds the current SARA balance in my MyKad?
Answer: Yes. Purchases are limited to essential goods from the 15 approved product categories only. Purchases of items outside these categories and purchases exceeding the current SARA balance in your MyKad must be paid by the recipient.

Question 16: If I do not use up my SARA for All credit balance in the current month, can the balance be carried forward to the following months?
Answer: Yes. Unused SARA credit balance will be carried forward to the following months. However, any credit balance not fully used by the end of the program period on 31 December 2026 will be used for other social protection programs in the following year.

Question 17: Does the SARA for All credit have an expiry date?
Answer: Yes. The validity period or expiry date of the SARA for All credit is 31 December 2026.

Question 18: If a SARA for All recipient is in prison, a rehabilitation centre, remand, another government care institution, or is ill, disabled, or has passed away, can a representative receive the SARA on their behalf?
Answer: No. SARA for All is specifically for the individual concerned and payment at the counter requires the recipient's MyKad. Any purchase made by someone other than the recipient is under the responsibility of the recipient themselves.

Question 19: What should I do if my MyKad is damaged or lost?
Answer: If your MyKad is damaged or lost, you should immediately visit the nearest National Registration Department (JPN) for a MyKad replacement. Purchases using SARA for All can be made up to 31 December 2026 after your new MyKad has been issued.

Question 20: Who can I contact for more information about the SARA for All program?
Answer: More information about SARA for All can be obtained at:
- Official SARA Portal: https://sara.gov.my
- MyKasih Helpline: 03-7720 1800

Question 21: I have a supermarket or grocery store and am interested in becoming a merchant partner for the SARA 2026 program. Who should I contact?
Answer: You can contact MyKasih Foundation via email at merchant@mykasih.com for further information. Merchant partner selection is subject to the terms and conditions set by MyKasih Foundation.

Question 22: How long does it take for a merchant partner application to be approved?
Answer: Once the complete application with all required documents is received by MyKasih Foundation, a period of 2 weeks is needed for the application to be approved, subject to the terms and conditions set.
