SARA UNTUK SEMUA — QUICK REFERENCE CARD

---

KEY DATES
- Program announced by the Prime Minister
- Credit date: 9 February 2026
- Spending period: 9 February 2026 to 31 December 2026
- Expiry: 31 December 2026 (unused balance goes to other social protection programs)
- Eligibility data cutoff: 23 January 2026 (JPN MyKad holder data)

---

AMOUNT
- RM100 one-off per eligible individual
- A household of 5 eligible adults receives RM500 total
- Existing SARA STR recipients get their regular credit PLUS the additional RM100 one-off

---

ELIGIBILITY
- Malaysian citizen (warganegara Malaysia)
- Aged 18 and above in 2026 (born 2008 or earlier)
- Must hold a MyKad
- Automatic qualification — no application needed
- Based on JPN data as of 23 January 2026

---

15 APPROVED PRODUCT CATEGORIES (Kategori Barangan yang Diluluskan)

1. Beras (Rice)
2. Roti (Bread)
3. Telur (Eggs)
4. Minyak Masak (Cooking Oil)
5. Tepung (Flour)
6. Biskut (Biscuits)
7. Mi (Noodles)
8. Minuman (Beverages) — susu, teh, kopi, susu bayi
9. Makanan Dalam Tin (Canned Food) — sardin, tuna
10. Bahan Perasa (Seasonings) — sos, kicap, gula, garam
11. Produk Kebersihan Diri (Personal Care) — ubat gigi, tuala wanita, sabun, lampin bayi
12. Barangan Kebersihan Rumah (Household Cleaning) — sabun pencuci baju, pinggan mangkuk, lantai
13. Ubat-Ubatan Di Kaunter (Over-the-Counter Medicine) — approved by KKM
14. Makanan Sejuk Beku (Frozen Food)
15. Barangan Persekolahan (School Supplies) — pakaian seragam, kasut, beg sekolah, alat tulis

Items are labelled with the MyKasih label on the price shelf tag.

---

PURCHASE PROCESS (Kaedah Pembelian)

Step 1: Select items from the 15 approved categories with MyKasih label
Step 2: Show MyKad to cashier for verification
Step 3: Cashier scans barcodes of approved items
Step 4: Pay using MyKad to confirm transaction
Step 5: Keep receipt for balance and transaction reference

---

BALANCE CHECK METHODS (Semakan Baki)

1. Portal: https://sara.gov.my
2. App: MyKasih app (Google Play Store / App Store)
3. Receipt: Shows opening balance, purchase amount, and final balance

---

IMPORTANT RULES

- Credit is non-transferable — only the MyKad holder can use it
- No proxy or representative (wakil) is allowed
- Excess purchases beyond SARA balance must be paid by the recipient
- Unused balance carries forward monthly until 31 December 2026
- Lost or damaged MyKad: visit JPN for replacement, then use by 31 December 2026
- No MyKad yet: visit JPN to obtain one, then use by 31 December 2026
